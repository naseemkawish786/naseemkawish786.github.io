About Wild Laughs

Our Story

Wild Laughs started with one simple idea: animals make the world happier — and smarter. From Skardu, in the mountains of Gilgit-Baltistan, we create family-friendly videos that mix genuine laughter with true knowledge. Every video opens with an impossible-looking moment, tells a tiny story, and teaches real facts about the animal on screen.

What We Post

  • Funny Shorts — 40 seconds of pure animal joy, twice a day.
  • A daily long video — amazing animal stories and facts, about 15 minutes.
  • A pet information series — breeds, pet health basics and building trust with your animals.
  • A daily community post — polls, teasers and fun facts. You help choose tomorrow's star!
