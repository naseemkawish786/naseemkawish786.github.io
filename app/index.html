Get the Wild Laughs App

This website is also an installable web app. Add Wild Laughs to your home screen and open your daily funny animals with one tap — no app store, no big download.

✅ You already have the app installed — enjoy!

Why install it?

  • One tap from your home screen to the latest videos and Shorts.
  • Fast and light — pages you have visited open even on slow connections, and many work offline.
  • No account, no cost — it is simply this website, dressed as an app.

Install on Android (Chrome)

  1. If the Install button above appears, just tap it — done!
  2. If not: open Chrome's menu (the ⋮ three dots at the top right).
  3. Tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
  4. Confirm with Install — the paw icon lands on your home screen.

Install on iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Choose "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
